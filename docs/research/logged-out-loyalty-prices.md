# Which Chains show their Loyalty price on public, logged-out pages?

Research for ticket [#30](https://github.com/exilonX/cart-compare/issues/30) on map [#1](https://github.com/exilonX/cart-compare/issues/1). Desk research done on 2026-10-21. Terms follow the glossary in `CONTEXT.md`: Chain, Shelf price, Loyalty price (the price advertised to every member, one number per product) and Personalised offer. This note builds on [#9 legal exposure of collection](https://github.com/exilonX/cart-compare/blob/research/legal-collection/docs/research/legal-collection.md), which checked Lidl, Kaufland and Penny, and on [#28 Loyalty price uniformity](https://github.com/exilonX/cart-compare/blob/research/loyalty-price-uniformity/docs/research/loyalty-price-uniformity.md), which read each scheme's terms. It does not repeat their findings.

**How the pages were read.** For each of the five Chains not covered by #9, we opened a handful of public pages one at a time on 2026-10-21: e-shop listing and product pages, weekly and member offer pages, and leaflet pages. We used a text-extraction tool, without logging in, creating an account, adding anything to a basket or opening any app. Quotes are in Romanian with an English translation. The tool reads the text the server sends. It does not run JavaScript, so labels drawn as images or loaded later by scripts can be missing (see [Gaps](#gaps-and-uncertainties)). Profi answered HTTP 403 and we did not try to get past it. Our tool could not reach web.archive.org or archive.ph, so we have no archived fallback for Profi.

## Answer

- **Logged-out Loyalty prices can be collected for five Chains, and probably a sixth, but not yet for all eight.** Lidl, Kaufland and Penny show the Loyalty price as a number on their weekly offer pages ([#9](https://github.com/exilonX/cart-compare/blob/research/legal-collection/docs/research/legal-collection.md)). Carrefour does the same in its e-shop. Mega Image shows only the size of the member discount. Auchan lists its member offers with prices, but the page text does not say which price is the member price. Profi and Selgros could not be checked. [Table](#all-8-chains).
- **Carrefour: yes, as a number, for products in an Act for Good offer.** The product page shows the Shelf price, then "PRET CU ACT FOR GOOD" ("price with Act for Good") and a second, lower price ([Twix product page](https://carrefour.ro/produse/baton-ciocolata-twix-3-buc-150g-19-14525167)). In the weekly offers listing, the tile carries an "Act for good" label ([weekly offers](https://carrefour.ro/campanii/oferte-saptamanale)). Carrefour also announces category-wide coupons "cu contul tau AFG" ("with your AFG account") that have no price per product ([AFG coupons page](https://carrefour.ro/campanii/reduceri-de-gama-afg)).
- **Mega Image: the discount, not the price.** Products in a member offer carry a badge such as "CONNECT -20%" or "CONNECT -95Lei", next to the Shelf price ([Morcovi product page](https://www.mega-image.ro/Fructe-si-legume-proaspete/Legume-proaspete/Morcovi/Morcovi/p/67178); [Connect offers page](https://www.mega-image.ro/cardul-connect)). The site says the reduced price is shown only in the basket of a logged-in customer with a linked Connect card. A Loyalty price can be worked out from the badge, but Mega Image does not show it as a number.
- **Auchan: probably, for MyCLUB offers, but without a label we could read.** A logged-out page lists "Toate ofertele MyCLUB Auchan" ("All MyCLUB Auchan offers"): 1,396 products, each with a lower price and a higher one ([MyCLUB offers page](https://www.auchan.ro/promotii/myclub-auchan)). Nothing in the page text marks the lower price as the member price. Auchan says it marks these offers with "semnalistica digitala in pagina de produs" ("digital signage on the product page") ([MyCLUB page](https://www.auchan.ro/my-club-auchan)). Our tool did not capture that signage.
- **Profi: not established.** The whole site answered HTTP 403 to our client ([home page](https://www.profi.ro/), [offers](https://www.profi.ro/oferte/)). The App Store listing describes "oferte saptamanale exclusive" ("exclusive weekly offers") inside the app ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ro/app/profi/id1569196033)). We found no logged-out page that shows them.
- **Selgros: not established.** Its "Card & App" page invites logged-out visitors to choose a store and "Vezi ofertele cu APP" ("see the offers with APP") ([Card & App](https://www.selgros.ro/card-app)). The listing loads in the browser and showed no products or prices to our tool. Product pages served no price either ([example](https://www.selgros.ro/exploreaza-sortimentul-selgros/product/diamant-briliant-zahar-1kg-1072384)). Every Selgros buyer needs a card anyway ([#28](https://github.com/exilonX/cart-compare/blob/research/loyalty-price-uniformity/docs/research/loyalty-price-uniformity.md)).
- **Scope is the same wherever a Loyalty price shows: only products in a current member offer.** No Chain shows a member price across its full range. Carrefour, Mega Image and Auchan show the Shelf price for the full e-shop range, and their member labels sit on a small subset. At Carrefour, 8 of roughly 300 visible tiles in the weekly offers carried "Act for good" ([weekly offers](https://carrefour.ro/campanii/oferte-saptamanale)).

### Notes per Chain

**Carrefour.**

- **Product page.** For "Baton ciocolata Twix, 3 buc, 150g" the page shows "11 Lei 79" (78.6 lei/kg), then "PRET CU ACT FOR GOOD 9 Lei 39" (62.6 lei/kg), with a "-20%" badge ([product page](https://carrefour.ro/produse/baton-ciocolata-twix-3-buc-150g-19-14525167)). The page does not say who qualifies for the price.
- **Weekly offers.** The weekly offers page ("Oferte valabile in perioada 14 -27 Octombrie", "offers valid 14–27 October") shows the same pair on tiles labelled "Act for good". Examples are Nesquik Alphabet (16.19 / 12.95 lei), Mars and Twix (11.79 / 9.39 lei), Leibniz Fun Bites (14.49 / 12.29 lei) and Pfanner Ice Tea. For Pfanner Ice Tea the tile adds "Pret + garantie 5.89 LEI" and "Pret AFG + garantie 4.79 LEI" ("price + deposit"; "AFG price + deposit") ([weekly offers](https://carrefour.ro/campanii/oferte-saptamanale)).
- **Products without an offer.** The first tiles of the grocery listing carry no Act for Good label and one price each ([grocery listing](https://carrefour.ro/bacanie-carrefour)).
- **Online and store prices may differ.** Every product page carries: "Pretul indicat pe site poate sa difere de pretul din magazinele Carrefour" → "The price shown on the site may differ from the price in Carrefour stores" ([product page](https://carrefour.ro/produse/baton-ciocolata-twix-3-buc-150g-19-14525167)).
- **Category coupons.** A separate page for 21–27 October announces "Cupon pana la 30% la cumparaturile facute cu contul tau AFG" → "Coupon of up to 30% on purchases made with your AFG account". The coupons are listed by category, for example "Cupon 30% la textile de casa Tex", "Cupon 25% la bagaje" and "Cupon 30% la Sensodyne" ("30% coupon on Tex home textiles", "25% coupon on luggage", "30% coupon on Sensodyne"). The product tiles on this page show two prices and a percentage, with no AFG label. The tile percentages do not always match the coupon (for example -25% on a Tex bed set under a 30% Tex coupon) ([AFG coupons page](https://carrefour.ro/campanii/reduceri-de-gama-afg)).
- **The AFG code is in the app only.** The Act for Good FAQ says "Codul Act for Good îl poți regăsi doar în aplicația Carrefour" → "You can find the Act for Good code only in the Carrefour app" ([FAQ](https://carrefour.ro/intrebari-frecvente-reclamatii/act_for_good/)).
- **Leaflets.** We found no current leaflet on the catalogues page. It shows campaign banners only ([catalogues](https://carrefour.ro/corporate/cataloage)).

**Mega Image.**

- **Product page.** "Gusturi romanesti Morcovi" shows the badge "CONNECT -20%", the unit price "3,99 Lei/kg" and the price "2 00 Lei". The page shows no second price ([product page](https://www.mega-image.ro/Fructe-si-legume-proaspete/Legume-proaspete/Morcovi/Morcovi/p/67178)). It carries this disclaimer: "In cazul promotiilor/ofertelor cardului Connect, pretul produsului este afisat cu reducerea aplicata doar in cosul de cumparaturi, in conditiile in care clientul este logat in contul inregistrat Mega Image si are asociat cardul Connect inscris in programul de loialitate." → "For Connect card promotions or offers, the product price is shown with the discount applied only in the shopping basket, provided the customer is logged in to a registered Mega Image account and has linked a Connect card enrolled in the loyalty programme." The price on the page is therefore the Shelf price.
- **Connect offers page.** "Promotii pentru membri CONNECT" ("Promotions for CONNECT members") lists products with badges in percent or in lei. Examples are "CONNECT -50%" on a set of towels shown at 60.00 lei, and "CONNECT -95Lei" on a bag shown at 152.59 lei ([Connect offers](https://www.mega-image.ro/cardul-connect)).
- **Personalised layers.** The same page had a notice: "Momentan întâmpinăm o problemă tehnică" ("we are currently having a technical problem"), affecting "ofertele personalizate și afișarea informațiilor Card Connect în contul online" ("personalised offers and the display of Card Connect information in the online account"). It added that "Reducerea la total coș (1%, 2% sau 3%) este disponibilă și se aplică în continuare la casele de marcat" ("the basket-total discount (1%, 2% or 3%) is available and still applies at the tills"). Under the glossary, that basket-total discount is a Personalised offer ([Connect offers](https://www.mega-image.ro/cardul-connect)).
- **Leaflet.** The "Revista promotionala" leaflet page loaded no readable leaflet text for our tool ([leaflets](https://www.mega-image.ro/promotii-online/reviste-promo-online); [viewer](https://www.mega-image.ro/promotii-online/reviste-promo-online/revista-promo)).

**Auchan.**

- **MyCLUB offers page.** The page said "Oferte valabile in perioada 30.09.2026 - 20.10.2026*, în limita stocurilor disponibile" ("offers valid 30.09–20.10.2026, while stocks last"), with fresh categories valid 14–20.10.2026. It listed "1396 de produse" ("1,396 products"). Example tiles: "Poale-n brau Auchan, 150 g" at 2,99 lei beside 5,59 lei; "Saleuri cu chimen Romcherie, 180 g" at 3,22 lei beside 9,69 lei ([MyCLUB offers](https://www.auchan.ro/promotii/myclub-auchan)).
- **The cycle had ended.** We read the page on 21.10, a day after the stated end. That day the Poale-n brau product page showed only 5,59 lei ([product page](https://www.auchan.ro/poale-n-brau-auchan-150-g/p)). The Saleuri page showed 7,09 lei beside 10,99 lei ([product page](https://www.auchan.ro/saleuri-cu-chimen-romcherie-180-g/p)).
- **No label on product pages either.** Neither product block carried a MyCLUB label in its text. Both pages carry a general MyCLUB disclaimer: "Beneficiile se acorda in limita a 12 unitati / card client o singura data in perioada promotiei" → "Benefits are given up to 12 units per customer card, once in the promotion period".
- **Top deals.** The leaflet "Top Deals" page shows the same two-price tiles under the same disclaimer ([top deals](https://www.auchan.ro/promotii/catalog-auchan/top-deals)).
- **Auchan's own description.** MyCLUB offers are "evidentiate prin etichete la raft si semnalistica digitala in pagina de produs" → "marked by shelf labels and digital signage on the product page" ([MyCLUB page](https://www.auchan.ro/my-club-auchan)). The terms say the same ([MyCLUB terms §5.1](https://www.auchan.ro/legal/termeni-si-conditii-myclub-auchan)).

**Selgros.**

- **Offers page.** "Oferte TOP" asks the visitor to "selectezi magazinul tău" ("select your store"). It says "Prețul este exprimat în lei și include TVA" ("the price is in lei and includes VAT"), but showed no products ([offers](https://www.selgros.ro/oferte)).
- **App offers.** The Card & App page says "Selectează magazinul tău pentru a vizualiza promoțiile pentru utilizatorii aplicației" → "Select your store to see the promotions for app users". It links to a filtered listing (`lf_labels=isApp`) ([Card & App](https://www.selgros.ro/card-app)). That listing, with and without a store parameter, showed no products to our tool ([listing](https://www.selgros.ro/exploreaza-sortimentul-selgros?&lf_labels=isApp)).
- **Catalogues.** The catalogues page links to assortment catalogues hosted on Yumpu, for example the 2026 meat catalogue. The Yumpu text available to our tool was only the title ([catalogues](https://www.selgros.ro/cataloage); [meat catalogue](https://www.yumpu.com/ro/document/read/67944690/catalog-carne-selgros-2026)).

**Profi.**

- **Site.** [profi.ro](https://www.profi.ro/) and [profi.ro/oferte](https://www.profi.ro/oferte/) answered HTTP 403. Two searches found no first-party leaflet hosted elsewhere.
- **App.** The App Store listing, by developer "Delhaize Group", version 26.6.1, says: "Acestia pot beneficia de oferte saptamanale exclusive, acces la promotiile din magazinele Profi" → "They can get exclusive weekly offers and access to promotions in Profi stores" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ro/app/profi/id1569196033)).

## Gaps and uncertainties

- **Text extraction misses what scripts draw.** Our tool reads served HTML without running JavaScript.
  - Lidl's Lidl Plus page and Selgros's listings showed product teasers or nothing, but no prices ([Lidl Plus weekly offers](https://www.lidl.ro/c/ofertele-saptamanale-lidl-plus/a10105244)). #9 found Lidl's label in page data.
  - Auchan's "digital signage" did not appear in the text.
  - A person should open the Auchan MyCLUB offers page, the Selgros app offers listing and the Mega Image leaflet in an ordinary browser, logged out, and record what they see.
- **Profi is unverified.** We have no live page and no archived copy. Whether Profi shows app offer prices anywhere without the app is unknown.
- **Auchan's lower price is not labelled in the text.** The MyCLUB offers page is titled as member offers, and Auchan's terms say MyCLUB discounts are for every cardholder ([#28](https://github.com/exilonX/cart-compare/blob/research/loyalty-price-uniformity/docs/research/loyalty-price-uniformity.md)). So the lower price is most likely the Loyalty price. But we could not rule out that some tiles carry a general promotion or a bonus-on-card offer, where the shelf price does not drop. The page was also stale: on 21.10 it still showed the cycle that ended on 20.10.
- **Mega Image shows dates that had passed.** The Morcovi page showed the badge with "De la 07.10.2026 la 14.10.2026" and "Promotii valabile din 08/10/26 pana in 14/10/26" on 21.10. It is unclear whether the badge was still live or left over. Working out a Loyalty price from the badge also needs a rule for rounding and for items sold by weight.
- **Carrefour's AFG coupons are not clearly a Loyalty price or a Personalised offer.** They are announced publicly for every AFG account, but they are category percentages, and the coupon code lives in the app. The glossary defines a Loyalty price as obtained by identifying at the till, and lists "targeted coupons" as a Personalised offer. These coupons sit between the two.
- **Online prices may not equal store prices.** Carrefour says so on every product page. Mega Image says product labels in store take precedence over the site ([Morcovi product page](https://www.mega-image.ro/Fructe-si-legume-proaspete/Legume-proaspete/Morcovi/Morcovi/p/67178)). We did not compare e-shop Loyalty prices with leaflets or shelf labels.
- **One day, a few pages.** Each Chain was checked on a handful of pages on 2026-10-21. We did not measure how many products carry a member price each week, beyond the counts above.
- **Leaflets were mostly not readable.** Carrefour's catalogues page showed no leaflet. Mega Image's leaflet viewer and Selgros's Yumpu catalogues gave no readable text. A Chain's leaflet may show member prices that its website does not.
