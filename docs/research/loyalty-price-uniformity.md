# Is a Chain's Loyalty price the same for every cardholder, or personalised?

Research for ticket [#28](https://github.com/exilonX/cart-compare/issues/28) on map [#1](https://github.com/exilonX/cart-compare/issues/1). Desk research done on 2026-10-21. Terms follow the glossary in `CONTEXT.md` (Chain, Shelf price, Loyalty price, Reported price). This note builds on [#9 legal exposure of collection](https://github.com/exilonX/cart-compare/blob/research/legal-collection/docs/research/legal-collection.md), [#18 comparison-country data](https://github.com/exilonX/cart-compare/blob/research/comparison-country-data/docs/research/comparison-country-data.md) and [#7 Romanian shoppers](https://github.com/exilonX/cart-compare/blob/research/romanian-shoppers/docs/research/romanian-shoppers.md). It does not repeat their findings.

**How the sources were read.** Each scheme's Romanian terms and the Chain's own help pages were fetched once, by hand, on 2026-10-21, without logging in, creating an account or opening any app. Terms published as PDFs (Lidl Plus, PENNY Card, the Carrefour app) were converted to text and read in full. HTML pages were read through a text-extraction tool. Quotes are in Romanian with an English translation. Profi's site and two Selgros pages answered HTTP 403, and we did not try to get past that. EUR-Lex returned empty pages, so the Commission's guidance was read in its German Official Journal version, from a copy hosted by the Austrian Chamber of Commerce (WKO).

## Answer

- **Every scheme whose terms we could read has a layer of member offers that it describes as open to all members and announces publicly.** That covers Lidl Plus (whose terms allow campaigns for all users or for chosen groups), Kaufland Card XTRA, PENNY Card, Carrefour Act for Good, Mega Image Connect and Auchan MyCLUB. These offers appear in the leaflet, on the website or on a shelf label, and the member gets them by identifying at the till. Kaufland's terms say its "Ofertele speciale dedicate tuturor utilizatorilor" ("special offers for all users") are flagged on the shelf ([§5.1](https://www.kaufland.ro/regulament-privind-protectia-datelor/termeni-conditii-kaufland-card.html)). Auchan's member discounts are "destinate tuturor Clientilor care detin un Card de fidelitate Auchan" ("for all customers who hold an Auchan loyalty card") ([§5.1](https://www.auchan.ro/legal/termeni-si-conditii-myclub-auchan)). Mega Image's are "valabile pentru toti detinatorii de card Connect" ("valid for all Connect card holders") ([cap. 18](https://www.mega-image.ro/termeni-si-conditii)). [Per-scheme table](#per-scheme-findings).
- **Every scheme also has personalised layers on top.** Lidl's Romanian terms (v3.0, July 2026) contain the same clause as the German ones: "este posibil ca anumite grupuri de utilizatori să primească procente diferite de reducere pentru aceeași categorie de produse" ("certain groups of users may receive different discount percentages for the same product category"), including "pentru a testa" ("to test") offers ([Lidl Plus terms §1, §4.3](https://assets.leaflets.schwarz/leaflets/pdfs/449530ec-2637-11ee-ba1f-fa163dadd92e/Lidl-Plus-Termeni-si-conditii-08.pdf)). Kaufland, Carrefour, Mega Image, Auchan and PENNY profile members who consent, and send them personalised coupons or vouchers. Points, bonus and spend-threshold schemes give each member a different effective price. At Mega Image, points set a monthly percentage discount for each member ([cap. 18 E.3](https://www.mega-image.ro/termeni-si-conditii)).
- **So a logged-in view is not "the" Loyalty price.** The apps mix the offers for all members with coupons "disponibile pentru tine" ("available for you") ([Lidl](https://www.lidl.ro/c/lidl-plus-cum-sa-folosesti-lidl-plus/s10020928)). Carrefour's own FAQ says "Ulterior promoțiile vor fi personalizate dacă ți-ai dat acceptul pentru profilare" ("later, promotions will be personalised if you consented to profiling") ([Carrefour FAQ](https://carrefour.ro/intrebari-frecvente-reclamatii/act_for_good/)). This is one more reason, on top of #9's legal ones, to read Loyalty prices from public sources and not from an account.
- **EU guidance draws the same line.** The Commission says Article 6a of the Price Indication Directive does not apply to "echte personalisierte Preisermäßigungen" ("genuinely personalised price reductions"). It does apply to member offers announced to consumers generally, such as "Dieses Wochenende ausschließlich für Treuekunden 20 % ermäßigt" ("20% off this weekend for loyalty customers only") ([Notice 2021/C 526/02, §2.3](https://www.wko.at/oe/handel/lebensmittelhandel/ek-leitlinien-preisauszeichnung.pdf)). An announced member price is therefore treated like a public price claim. A personalised coupon is not.
- **Verdict: yes, the Loyalty price can be published as one number per product, Chain and week, under four conditions.**
  1. It is defined as the price the Chain advertises to **all** members of its scheme.
  2. The member gets it only by identifying with the card or app at the till, or by activating an offer that every member can see.
  3. It is read from public sources: leaflets, logged-out offer pages and shelf labels.
  4. It excludes personalised coupons and vouchers, discounts given to test groups, redemptions of points, bonus or vouchers, spend-based or basket-wide percentage discounts, birthday and welcome vouchers, and any quantity above a per-receipt limit. Auchan's limit is 12 uses of an offer, or 36 for drinks ([§5.4](https://www.auchan.ro/legal/termeni-si-conditii-myclub-auchan)).
- **Three exceptions need decisions.**
  - **Selgros** lets no one into its stores without a Selgros card ([art. 2.1](https://www.selgros.ro/conditii-vanzare-cumparare)). By the glossary's definition, Selgros therefore has no Shelf price. Its app-only offers form a second tier.
  - **Profi**'s app terms could not be read (HTTP 403), so the Profi verdict is provisional.
  - **Carrefour**'s terms let it set "tipul, durata și valoarea" ("the type, duration and value") of benefits "pentru fiecare Client Participant" ("for each Participating Customer") unilaterally ([app terms, Act for Good](https://carrefour.ro/corporate/aplicatia-carrefour/termeni-si-conditii/)). Its offers for all members are uniform today, but that should be re-checked.

### What the EU guidance says

The Commission's notice on Article 6a of Directive 98/6/EC has a section 2.3, "Kundentreueprogramme und personalisierte Preisermäßigungen" (loyalty programmes and personalised price reductions) ([OJ C 526/130, 29.12.2021, pp. 136–137](https://www.wko.at/oe/handel/lebensmittelhandel/ek-leitlinien-preisauszeichnung.pdf); [EUR-Lex record, CELEX 52021XC1229(06)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:52021XC1229(06))). It says three things:

- **Long-running loyalty discounts and points are outside Article 6a.** "Artikel 6a … gilt nicht für Kundentreueprogramme des Verkäufers wie Rabattkarten oder Gutscheine, mit denen dem Verbraucher ein Preisnachlass auf alle Produkte … während längerer ununterbrochener Zeiträume (z. B. 6 Monate, 1 Jahr) eingeräumt oder die Akkumulierung von Bonuspunkten … ermöglicht wird" → "Article 6a … does not apply to the seller's loyalty programmes, such as discount cards or vouchers, that give the consumer a reduction on all products … for longer uninterrupted periods (e.g. 6 months, 1 year), or that allow bonus points to be accumulated …".
- **Genuinely personalised reductions are outside it too.** It "gilt auch nicht für echte personalisierte Preisermäßigungen" → "also does not apply to genuinely personalised price reductions". The examples given are a voucher earned from past purchases, a welcome or birthday discount, and a discount not announced before the purchase.
- **Member offers announced to many members are inside it.** Article 6a applies where reductions, "wenngleich als personalisiert dargestellt", are "in Wirklichkeit im Allgemeinen angeboten bzw. angekündigt" → "although presented as personalised, are in reality offered or announced generally". One example is "Dieses Wochenende ausschließlich für Treuekunden 20 % ermäßigt" ("20% off this weekend for loyalty customers only"). The reason given: "ist der Code/das Kundentreueprogramm für viele Kunden oder die Mehrheit der Kunden zugänglich" → "the code or loyalty programme is accessible to many or most customers". The seller must then show the lowest price of the last 30 days as the prior price.

The Chains' weekly offers for all members match the third case. PENNY already shows "preț minim ultimele 30 de zile" ("lowest price in the last 30 days") next to its card prices ([#9](https://github.com/exilonX/cart-compare/blob/research/legal-collection/docs/research/legal-collection.md)). Coupons, test-group discounts, birthday and welcome vouchers, and points redemptions match the first two cases.

## Implications for the glossary term Loyalty price

- **The current definition is too broad.** "The price shown only to holders of a chain's account, app or loyalty card" also covers personalised coupons and test-group prices. These differ between members, so they cannot be one number. A tighter wording, offered for the domain-model owner to decide on:
  > **Loyalty price**: The price a Chain advertises to every holder of its loyalty card or app, which they get by identifying at the till. It excludes personalised coupons and vouchers, points or bonus redemptions, and spend-based discounts.
- **A term for what is excluded would help.** For example, *Personalised offer*: "a discount a Chain gives to one member or a group of members, based on their profile, spending or a test. Not part of the index." It keeps the method page honest about what the index leaves out.
- **The Loyalty price is a floor for some members, not for all.** A member with a coupon, a points voucher or a Mega Image percentage level pays less than the published Loyalty price. A member who buys more than a per-receipt limit, such as Auchan's 12 uses, pays more for the extra units. The method page should say this.
- **Selgros breaks the Shelf price definition.** No one can buy at Selgros without its card ([art. 2.1](https://www.selgros.ro/conditii-vanzare-cumparare)). The project has to choose between three options:
  1. Treat the Selgros card as an access condition rather than a loyalty scheme. The card-holder price is then Selgros's "Shelf price" and app offers are its Loyalty price.
  2. Show Selgros with a Loyalty price only.
  3. Drop Selgros from the Basket ranking.
- **One Loyalty price per Chain is not one per store.** Kaufland personalises by "magazinul preferat" ("preferred store") (§5), and Selgros shows app offers per store ([Card & App](https://www.selgros.ro/card-app)). Uniform across members does not mean uniform across stores. The Bucharest-first scope limits this, but does not remove it.

## Gaps and uncertainties

- **Profi:** the app terms and the loyalty page answered HTTP 403. Our fetch tool could not reach the web archive. The Profi row rests on the App Store listing and a search-engine summary, so a person has to read the terms in a browser.
- **Selgros:** we found no card or app regulament. The coupons page and the app page answered HTTP 403. Whether Selgros personalises coupons is unknown.
- **Carrefour:** the app terms say they were last updated on 18.03.2021, but they also mention points expiring in 2022, so parts are stale. The FAQ is undated. We could not tell whether personalised promotions ("ulterior", "later") have started.
- **Lidl:** the terms allow group-specific discount rates without limiting them to app coupons. Nothing we read rules out that a "cu Lidl Plus" leaflet price could vary by user group. The [Lidl help article on coupons](https://serviciul-clienti.lidl.ro/SelfServiceRO/s/article/Ce-sunt-cupoanele) did not render without JavaScript.
- **PENNY:** the regulament does not mention personalised coupons, but it allows profiling (§8.5) and vouchers on terms PENNY sets (§6.2). What the app shows each member was not observed, because we did not use the app.
- **Mega Image:** chapter numbering in the terms is inconsistent (18 vs 17), and the page has no update date. The thresholds for the E.3 percentage levels are not in the terms. #9 cites a 1–3% site banner.
- **Auchan:** the terms are undated. The terms put the 12-use limit per receipt ("oferta/bon"), while the MyCLUB page puts it per card ("oferta/card client").
- **Logged-out visibility** of Loyalty prices at Carrefour, Mega Image, Auchan and Selgros was not checked. Mega Image and Auchan say they mark member offers online ("CONNECT"; "semnalistica digitala in pagina de produs"). #9 checked only Lidl, Kaufland and Penny.
- **EU guidance:** the German Official Journal text was read from a WKO-hosted copy because EUR-Lex returned empty pages. The English version was not compared.
- **Romanian law:** we did not read the Romanian transposition of Article 6a, nor any ANPC guidance on loyalty prices. Nor did we check the Consumer Rights Directive duty to disclose personalised pricing for online sales. These are questions for the Romanian IT lawyer already named in #9.
- **Quotes from HTML pages** were taken through a text-extraction tool. Quotes from the three PDFs were checked against the full extracted text.
